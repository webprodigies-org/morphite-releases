## Improved

- **Faster Windows workspaces.** Windows workspaces prepare faster, so you can get into work sooner.

## Fixed

- **Windows terminals close cleanly.** Closing a terminal finishes its session properly.
- **Coordinators keep moving.** A stopped worker no longer leaves the coordinator waiting.
- **Workers recover on their own.** A worker that switches branches can get back to work automatically.
