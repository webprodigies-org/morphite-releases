## Cool and fast while agents work

Morphite now uses far less graphics power, so your computer stays cooler and quieter while agents work. Browser tabs sleep when nobody's using them, and tabs your agents open work quietly in the background instead of popping open your panel.

## Long chats open instantly

A chat opens at once, however long it is.

## Fixed

- Your typing is never interrupted by agents working in the browser.
- The phone app connects reliably to large companies and long rooms, and when something's wrong it says clearly what to do.
