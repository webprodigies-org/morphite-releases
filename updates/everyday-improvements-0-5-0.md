## Improved

- **Menus stay within reach.** Live menus open over browser pages while you work.
- **Clearer merge notices.** Plain words make it easier to see what happened to your team’s work.
- **The Morphite bot works across your repositories.** Rooms can post as the bot from any linked repository.
- **A smoother reload.** Get back to your work with a calmer reload.

## Fixed

- **Clicks keep working.** Browser pages no longer block clicks on Morphite’s controls.
