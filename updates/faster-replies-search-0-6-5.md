## New

- **Search everything with ⌘K.** Find rooms, messages, settings and connections from one search box.
- **Find in chat with ⌘F.** Jump between matches in the conversation you're reading.
- **Split from a single panel.** Drag a tab to the top or bottom of a lone panel to split it, just like left and right.

## Improved

- **Much faster replies.** Every agent keeps working in the same session between your messages, so follow-ups start answering sooner.
- **Agents and your browser.** Agents always reach Morphite's browser and know which tab you're looking at.
- **A tidier sidebar.** Settings sits at the bottom of the sidebar, and your profile picture ends the company rail.
- **Steadier reconnects.** Remote connections and webhooks reconnect by themselves after your computer sleeps or changes networks.
- **Your Windows settings reach agents.** On Windows, agents see the user environment variables you set, even ones added after Morphite started.

## Fixed

- **No black browser tabs.** A browser tab shows its page again after you switch away and back.
- **The browser animation stops with the agent.** The "agent at work" frame goes away as soon as the agent stops.
- **New room opens your draft.** Starting a new room no longer asks whether to keep writing or start over.
- **No progress comments on GitHub.** Syncing a ticket with GitHub never posts its progress as a comment on the issue.
- **What’s New looks right on the Mac.** Its frosted header draws correctly again.
