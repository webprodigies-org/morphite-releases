## New

- Room settings: choose which MCP servers a room's agents may use, including locked ones (room ⋯ menu › Room settings).
- One compact, searchable server picker in Room settings and in an automation's "What it can do".
- Lock an MCP server from its Add/Edit dialog; locked servers show a lock beside their name.
- A room that just sent you a notification shows a filled circle in the sidebar until you open it.
- Videos and audio play right in the chat and the side panel. Files Morphite can't preview show a clean card with Show in Finder / File Explorer, and Open for documents.
- Brain: new pages ask for a "When to use" line so agents know when to open them, and agents now write it too.

## Fixed

- Browser sign-ins are kept even if Morphite is force-quit soon after signing in.
- Large images and videos no longer show "too large" in the side panel.
