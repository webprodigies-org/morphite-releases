## Branch any agent

Duplicate any worker with its whole conversation and its files, then send the copy a different direction. Click **Branch** on a worker in the Team panel. The original keeps going untouched, and the branch remembers everything it knew.

## Every company at a glance

The company rail now shows when agents are working in a company, and how many have finished since you last looked. Hover over a company to preview just the rooms that need you, laid out exactly like its sidebar, and click one to jump straight there.

## Notifications from all your companies

Switch between **This company** and **All companies**, with whatever needs you always at the top. Filter by company or by what needs you. Each company's notifications stay separate and are labelled with the company they belong to.

## Improved

- Switching companies is instant.
- Antigravity now runs on Windows and Linux.
- The Morphite bot posts on every repository where it's installed, even when a project also uses others.
- Agents keep the tools your shell sets up, like nvm and Homebrew, even when Morphite is opened from the Dock.
- The phone app shows the same version as Morphite.

## Fixed

- Amazon Bedrock lists its models in every Region, and tells you why when a key is rejected.
- "Answer with an agent" on a GitHub webhook now wakes an agent for each new issue.
- The chat box no longer has a folder button that looked like opening a project. Choose a project with New room's folder picker.
