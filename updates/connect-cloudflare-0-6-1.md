## Improved

- **Connect Cloudflare with one button.** In **Settings › Connections › Webhooks**, click **Connect Cloudflare**. Cloudflare opens beside your work with everything filled in: sign in or create a free account, click Create, and Morphite finishes the rest. Nothing to copy or paste.
- **A private sign-in.** Cloudflare opens in its own browser session that agents can't use, and Morphite clears it when you're done.

## Fixed

- **Agents never take your focus.** When an agent works in Morphite's browser, your window, page and cursor stay where you are.
- **Agents can't use your passkeys.** Pages an agent drives can't ask for a passkey; your own browsing keeps them.
- **Features stay on.** A computer left idle no longer turns your features off; Morphite renews your access in the background.
