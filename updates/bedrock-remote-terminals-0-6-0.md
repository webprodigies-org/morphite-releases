## New

- **Amazon Bedrock.** Connect Amazon Bedrock as an API provider, and choose your AWS Region in its connection panel.
- **Terminal agents on your other computers.** Watch a terminal agent that runs on a computer you control, in the same terminal view as your own. It keeps that computer's size and never resizes its terminal.
- **Type into them when you allow it.** On the computer that runs the agent, turn on **Settings › Devices › Allow terminal input from paired devices**. It's off until you turn it on, and the room notes which device typed and when.

## Improved

- **Automations on any computer.** Choose which of your computers runs an automation, and follow its runs there.
- **Safer pairing.** Pairing another computer from a copied link confirms with a six-digit code, and finishes only once that computer has saved it.
- **A steadier start.** The launch screen never hangs, and remote control starts by itself even when your Mac's Keychain answers late.

## Fixed

- **Focus comes back where you left it.** Closing a menu returns you to what you were doing, unless you moved on.
- **Panel tabs stay whole.** A tab scrolled into view is no longer clipped at fractional display scales.
- **New webhook automations open here.** A webhook's new automation opens on this computer even when another computer was shown.
